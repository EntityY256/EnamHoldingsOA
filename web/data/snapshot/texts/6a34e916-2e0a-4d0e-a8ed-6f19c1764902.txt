Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015

As per Regulation 33 of SEBI Listing Regulations:
The Board has approved the Unaudited Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results of the
Company for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026. In this regard, please find
enclosed the following:
• Unaudited Consolidated Financial Results in the prescribed form along with Limited

Statutory Auditors - Annexure A
• Unaudited Standalone Financial Results in the prescribed form along with Limited Review
Report thereon received from Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP, Statutory
Auditors - Annexure B

The Board has declared an Interim Dividend of ₹ 8/- (INR eight rupees only) per equity share
of the face value of ₹2/- each (i.e. 400% of the face value) for the Financial Year 2026-27.

In accordance with Regulation 42 of SEBI Listing Regulations, Thursday, November 05, 2026
has been fixed as the Record Date for ascertaining eligibility for the payment of Interim
Dividend declared by the Board for the Financial Year 2026-27. The Interim Dividend will be
paid on or after November 26, 2026.

review by the statutory auditors.
4. The Board of Directors of the Company declared an interim dividend of~ 8.00 per share
(400%) on equity shares of nominal value of~ 2.00 each for the financial year 2026-27.
5. Pursuant to a notification issued by the Ministry of Labour and Employment, existing
labour laws were consolidated into four Labour Codes ("New Labour Codes") with effect
from 2l5t November 2025 . The Group recognised the liability of~ 6.75 crores for the year
ended 31 st March 2026, disclosed as an "Exceptional Item".
The Group continues to monitor notifications, rules and clarifications issued by State
and/or Central Government authorities in relation to the New Labour Codes. Any financial
impact arising from such developments will be evaluated and accounted for in the period
in which they are notified.
6. The consolidated results include the financial results of three foreign subsidiaries -
Manchester Organics Limited, NFIL (UK) Limited and Navin Fluorine (Shanghai) Co.
Limited, a step-down foreign subsidiary - NFIL USA Inc., a 100% subsidiary of NFIL
(UK) Limited, two Indian subsidiaries - Sulakshana Securities Limited and Navin Fluorine
Advanced Sciences Limited and a joint venture company - Swamim Gujarat Fluorspar
Private Limited.
