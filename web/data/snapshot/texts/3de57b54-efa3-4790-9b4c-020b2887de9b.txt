6. We draw attention to Note 3 of the consolidated financial results wherein the Holding Company
had received an unfavourable arbitration award seeking payment of USO 490.32 million and
related costs and interest during the year ended March 31, 2026, for which appeals were filed
by the Holding Company. During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Singapore
International Commercial Court ("SICC") dismissed the Holding Company's appeal to set aside
the award and subsequently an enforcement order has been issued by the SICC pursuant to which
on 6th November, 2026, notices were served intimating attachment of the Holding Company's
shareholding in certain Singapore subsidiaries by the Court-appointed Sheriff. Based on the
advice of legal counsels, the Holding Company has filed notices of appeal before the Singapore
Court of Appeal ('Apex Court') and is required to file its written submissions by November 23,
2026. Further, the Holding Company is in the process of filing objections to the Enforcement
Order and proceedings and seeking a deferment of sale of shares from the Sheriff, considering
the ongoing appeal before the Apex Court. Pending the final outcome of the Apex Court and
Enforcement proceedings as mentioned in the aforesaid note, no adjustments have been made
in the consolidated financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 and the year to
date results for the period from April 01. 2026 to September 30, 2026.

NOTES TO THE CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL RESULTS - Q2 FY27
1. The above consolidated financial results of The Tata Power Company Limited ('the Holding Company') and its
subsidiaries (together referred to as 'Group'), associates and joint ventures were reviewed by the Audtt
Committee and approved by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on 11th November 2026.
2. The Holding Company supplied power from the Mundra Power Plant ('Plant') upto 30th June 2025, based on
the directions of the Ministry of Power ('MoP') under Section 11 of the Electricity Act, 2003 since 16th April
2023. Effective 3rd July 2025, the Holding Company temporarily suspended operations of the Plant to
undertake pending overhauling activities aimed at resolving existing technical issues. On 23rd March 2026,
with effect from 1st April 2025, the Holding Company executed the supplementary power purchase agreement
('SPPA') with Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited ('GUVNL'), with revised tariff and power supply framework ,
which superseded the earlier PPA for GUVNL's contracted capacity . While approvals from the remaining
procurers are in progress, the MoP issued directions under Section 11 on 22nd March 2026, permitting plant
operations from 1st April 2026 to 30th June 2026 with the terms of the SPPA which was extended up to 30th
September, 2026 by way of directions issued on 19th June 2026. Further on 25th September 2026, such
directions have been extended till 31st December 2026. Management expects completion of the SPPA with
the other procurers during this period .
3. On 26th September 2023 the Singapore International Arbitration Centre ('SIAC') published a liability award
whereby the SIAC held that the Holding Company was in breach of certain clauses of the Non-disclosure
agreements entered into with Kleros Capital Partners Limited ('Kleros') and of its contractual duty of good fatth
and confidence. Further, on 1st July 2025 and 27th August 2025, the SIAC published a quantum award and
final award (together referred to as 'Awards'), directing the Holding Company to pay Kleros damages for loss
of opportunity of USD 490.32 million with simple interest of 5.33% from 30th November 2020 and cost of SGD
11.34 million with simple interest of 5.33% from 1st July 2025.
On 23rd October 2025, the Holding Company had challenged the Awards before the Singapore International
Commercial Court ('SICC') by filing applications to setaside the Awards. However, on 26th August, 2026, the
SICC dismissed the Holding Company's applications to set aside these Awards ('SICC Award'), concludirg
that the SIAC had considered and determined the relevant issues and that there was no basis for the SICC to
re-examine those matters. Further, the SICC passed judgments on 17th September 2026 and 18th September
2026, for enforcement of the Quantum Award and the Final Award respectively. The Holding Company has
filed notices of appeal against the SICC Award before the Singapore Court of Appeal ('Apex Court') on 7th
September, 2026 which were accepted on 15th September, 2026. Pursuant to the directions issued by the
Court, the Holding Company is required to file its written submissions by 23rd November 2026, Kleros is
required to file its submissions by 14th December 2026, and the Holding Company is required to file its reply
submissions by 24th December 2026. The appeal has been listed for hearing on 19th January 2027.
Subsequently, in an ex parte application filed by Kleros before the SICC, an order dated 26th October 2026,
was passed directing officers of the Holding Company to appear before the Registrar on 8th December 2026
and provide details of the Holding Company's assets. Pursuant thereto, on 6th November 2026, notices were
served on the Holding Company's wholly owned Singapore subsidiaries, Tata Power International Pte. Limited
and Shira Investments Pte. Limited, intimating attachment of the Holding Company's shareholding in the
aforesaid entities by the Court-appointed Sheriff (Enforcement Officer) upto the value of SGD 830. 77 million.
The Sheriff also served a copy of the aforesaid order passed by the SICC. The Holding Company's
shareholding in these entities is pledged in favour of the consortium of lenders of the respective subsidiaries.
The Holding Company is in process of filing an objection to Enforcement proceedings . If Kleros disputes the
grounds of objection, the Sheriff will direct the objector to file an application for release of the Shares. The
Sheriff can only commence the sale or delivery process of the Shares after the Release Application is decided
by the SICC which is further appealable. Pending the adjudication of the appeal before the Court of Appeal,
the Holding Company will also seek deferment of sale of shares from the Sheriff.
