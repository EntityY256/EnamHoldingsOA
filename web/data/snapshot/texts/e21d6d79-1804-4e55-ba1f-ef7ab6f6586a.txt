About Laurus Labs
Laurus Labs is a research-driven pharmaceutical and biotechnology company committed to improving global health. It holds
a leadership position in developing and manufacturing select Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) and Finished Dosage
Forms (FDF) across anti-retroviral, oncology, cardiovascular, and gastro therapeutics. With strong backward integration and
stringent quality standards, Laurus has built a solid reputation for high-quality, innovative solutions.
The company offers end-to-end Contract Development and Manufacturing Organization (CDMO) services, supporting
innovators from early-stage development to commercial production. Laurus employs over 8,126 people, including 3,134+
scientists, and operates 15 facilities approved by global regulators like the USFDA, WHO, EMA, and more. Its “Smart and
Green” chemistry approach drives sustainable manufacturing and operational excellence.
Laurus Labs generated ₹6,813 crore in revenue in FY2026 and is listed on the BSE and NSE. The company is a certified
Great Place to Work and holds a “BBB” MSCI ESG rating, reflecting its commitment to transparency, integrity, and ESG
principles. It is widely recognized for upholding environmental stewardship and ethical business practices.
Expanding beyond small molecules, Laurus is enhancing its capabilities in biotechnology, large molecules, cell, and gene
therapies. Its diversified offerings span human and animal health APIs, intermediates, crop science, and specialty
ingredients for nutrition and cosmetics. Guided by the principle “Chemistry for Better Living,” Laurus remains dedicated to
advancing science for better global health outcomes. Corporate Identification No: L24239AP2005PLC047518.

Hyderabad, October 29, 2026: Laurus Labs Ltd. (Laurus BSE: 540222, NSE: LAURUSLABS, ISIN:
INE947Q01028), a leading research-driven pharmaceutical and biotechnology company, today
announced signing of a definite agreement for investments in to Galenvs Sciences for a majority
ownership, a Montréal, Québec, Canada-based biotechnology company specialising in precision
magnetic nanoparticles and DNA/RNA technologies for advanced diagnostics and therapeutics.
This marks an important step in Laurus Labs’ strategy to strengthen its capabilities in
oligonucleotide development and manufacturing, while enhancing its Contract Development and
Manufacturing Organization (CDMO) offerings.
The strategic investment is aimed at unlocking greater efficiency, scalability and technological
capabilities in RNA and oligonucleotide manufacturing, enabling Laurus Labs to expand its
presence in the rapidly growing field of DNA/RNA-based precision therapeutics. The combination
of Galenvs Sciences’ specialised technology platforms with Laurus Labs’ research, development
and manufacturing capabilities is expected to create differentiated solutions for customers
developing complex and next-generation therapeutics.
The signing of a definitive Term sheet Agreement on 29th October 2026, under which Laurus Labs
Ltd. acquiring 60% of the equity share capital, at a pre money valuation of USD 3.50 million subject
to due diligence. Laurus Labs propose to invest 3.75 million, which includes a primary infusion of
USD 2.75 million.
Dr. Satyanarayana Chava, Founder & Chief Executive Officer, Laurus Labs said, “The investment
in to Galenvs Sciences marks another pivotal step in Laurus Labs’ strategic evolution as a research-
driven organization that has consistently invested ahead of the curve. Galenvs brings differentiated
technology capabilities in oligonucleotides and precision magnetic nanoparticles, which we believe
can create significant value for our customers and strengthen our capabilities in TIDES. By
combining these technologies with our research, development and manufacturing strengths, we
aim to enable the development of advanced therapeutics more efficiently and at scale. Our
objective is to build differentiated capabilities that can help bring next-generation therapies to
patients at an affordable cost, while creating long-term value for our customers and stakeholders.”
