Annexure-I
Shri Anindya Biswas, DS & Director RCI is renowned for his R&D efforts towards
indigenous design, development and production hand holding of various state of
the art avionic sub-systems for different indigenous missiles developed in the
country.
He joined DRDO in the year 1992 and since then he has played a vital role
towards the indigenous design & development of several missile systems in
programme, mission and technology development modes. These include glide
bombs, anti-radiation missiles, surface to air missiles, very short-range air
defence, ballistic missile defence, anti-ship missiles, surface to surface long
range missiles, unmanned aerial vehicle launched missiles, anti-tank missiles,
etc.
He has worked towards establishing an agile work culture for the development of
several enabling and disruptive technologies required for futuristic weapon
systems. Several new efforts including task teams with a pool of young scientists,
continued academia support through resident postgraduate students & regular
startups/ entrepreneur outreach etc are contributing significantly in realizing cost
effective & futuristic solutions in short duration of time.
For his distinguished contributions, he has been conferred with DRDO Young
Scientists Award and Scientist of the Year Award. He has done his graduation in
Electronics and Telecommunications from Bengal Engineering College, Shibpur
and M. Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from IIT Kharagpur.
He is not related to any other director of the Company and does not hold shares
in the Company.
