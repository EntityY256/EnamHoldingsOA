Sub: Intimation under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)
Regulations, 2015 - Appointment of Interim Chief Executive Officer (“Interim CEO”) of the Bank
Pursuant to Regulation 30 and other applicable clauses of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we would like to inform that the Board of Directors of the Bank, at its
meeting held today, i.e., November 03, 2026, inter-alia considered and approved the following:
Appointment of Mr. Balaji Nuthalapadi (DIN: 08198456) as the Interim CEO of the Bank
Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board of Directors
has approved the appointment of Mr. Balaji Nuthalapadi (DIN: 08198456) Whole-Time Director (Executive
Director) of the Bank as the Interim CEO of the Bank which shall be subject to the approval from the Reserve
Bank of India (RBI).
The appointment of Mr. Balaji Nuthalapadi (DIN: 08198456) as the Interim CEO of the Bank, consequent to
the resignation of Mr. P. N. Vasudevan (DIN: 01550885) as the MD & CEO, shall take effect from the date
of RBI approval or such other date as may be stipulated by the RBI in this regard.
The Board placed on record its deep appreciation and sincere thanks to Mr. P. N. Vasudevan for his
invaluable contributions, exemplary leadership, and dedicated service to the Bank during his tenure as MD
& CEO. Upon receipt of RBI approval for the appointment of the Interim CEO of the Bank and in
consultation with Mr. P. N. Vasudevan, the Board will decide his relieving date.
The Board has also initiated the process of identifying a successor to Mr. P. N. Vasudevan and will submit
its recommendation to the RBI as per the applicable regulations. The details as required under Regulation
30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 read with SEBI Master
circular bearing reference No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, are
enclosed as Annexure.
This intimation is being submitted in furtherance of, and supersedes, the earlier disclosure filed by the
Bank on October 29, 2026.
